# QindaQt — Mineral Light

Original wallpaper collection: Mineral Light combines soft geometry, tactile mineral surfaces, and warm light. QindaPunk adds the user-requested cute, badass cyberpunk Linux penguin and rubber-duck humor.
Jade Fold is the balanced signature; Porcelain Dawn is the light variant;
Ink Tide is the dark variant. Each image leaves broad quiet space for work.

The companion icon palette uses ink `#172528`, jade `#70BFA5`, porcelain
`#F3EFE5`, apricot `#E8AE84`, blue `#739EBB`, and violet `#A69AC5`.
Icons use crisp silhouettes at small sizes; wallpapers carry texture and depth.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. These are original
generated assets, not photographs or third-party branded artwork. The native
files are 1672 × 941 PNGs; the requested 4K size was not returned by the tool.
