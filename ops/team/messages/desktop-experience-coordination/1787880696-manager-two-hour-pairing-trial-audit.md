# Two-hour pairing trial audit: keep the system, subordinate it to outcomes

- Time: 2026-08-27T19:31:36-06:00
- Trial window: 2026-08-27T17:28:41-06:00 through
  2026-08-27T19:28:41-06:00
- Decision: keep adaptive lead/partner/reviewer pairing where a concrete
  outcome benefits; do not maintain pairs or headcount without product work

The trial produced materially better product evidence than solo implementation.
It is retained under the outcome-first operating brief, not as a fixed staffing
scheme.

## Product evidence

- Notification partners found and repaired the exact `10 * 1800` outer-timeout
  budget with no cleanup margin, missing KF6 discovery in the live matrix,
  incompatible Qt 6.11 `QJsonArray::constFirst` use, overflow/focus failures,
  signal PID/session containment, snapshot identity, unique-locker ownership,
  restart guarding, restoration arming, and a stale contract comment. The
  repaired source received an independent exact-state PASS; fresh broad runtime
  qualification remains honestly open.
- Controls partners found and repaired missing error/busy/disabled visual
  states, color-only accessibility drift, invalid `TextField` content handling,
  precision-sensitive RGBA behavior, a clipped Apply control, and compact
  StateCard text collapsing to one character in two themes. The compact
  regression was expanded from one theme to all five and received an independent
  rereview PASS; regenerated visual qualification is currently running.
- Display partners found and repaired transaction timeout, disabled-snapshot
  fingerprint consistency, and the Fable transition audit's T1-T8/Q1-Q2
  counterexamples. The exact D1 candidate then passed focused and broad
  Debug/Release plus fresh ASan+UBSan gates; its immutable-commit Fable review is
  now live.
- The D0 lead/auditor pair immediately rejected unsafe assumptions about KWin
  backend capability probing, virtual-output naming, synchronous mutation, and
  output-priority ranges before runtime code was allowed to compile.

## Limits and operating correction

- This evidence shows that direct partners and independent reviewers can improve
  defect discovery and repair speed. It does not show that a fixed pair or a
  larger roster is always faster.
- Assignment records and completed handoffs are not liveness. Only a live
  process doing the named outcome is reported as working.
- Communication and review messages are enabling evidence, not product
  completion. Progress is counted only when behavior, tests, integration, and a
  finished user outcome agree.
- Capacity is refilled with the nearest valid whole outcome. If no safe outcome
  is ready, the slot stays empty rather than creating managerial microtasks.

The manager loop remains: assign one clear outcome, observe direct evidence,
remove obstacles, connect peers when useful, route exact defects back to the
same implementer, integrate a passing immutable commit immediately, and refill
only with real product work.
