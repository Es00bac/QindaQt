## Findings and repairs

1. Installed r42/r43 omit the October 21 refused-rack retry, meter snapshot
   identity and coordinator revision fixes. Their mechanisms can cause heap
   growth, unavailable applet controls and delayed graph updates. These earlier
   fixes were already on hub main and are part of the required delivery base.
2. The October 24 laptop Settings core (PID 1521064) is in `ppoll` under
   `wl_display_dispatch_queue`, reached from a Qt socket notifier; SIGABRT came
   from another same-user process. Both display and clipboard adapters used
   blocking dispatch. Their read callbacks now prepare/read once and dispatch
   pending events without waiting for future compositor traffic.
3. Installed Audio1 exposes its internal meters as application streams. The
   laptop had nine such rows ahead of real applications. Current owned bound
   stream IDs now exclude meters; an external capture stream with a meter-like
   name remains visible.
4. Raw virtual-strip proxies outlived daemon-side node removal and prevented
   recreation. The preserved October 21 draft now has verified deferred,
   rate-bounded recovery and teardown cancellation. Its original qinda worktree
   was left untouched.
5. Metering, recording and VBAN trusted plane/chunk pointers, byte lengths and
   ignored capture offsets. A shared private F32 stereo view bounds and splits
   valid ranges and rejects malformed metadata. VBAN playback bounds the
   64-bit request before narrowing; ring overflow preserves stereo pairs.
6. Settings focus registration treated `undefined` as a valid Item during row
   initialization/retirement. Device, stream and virtual-device sections now
   normalize missing actions to null. Churn and incomplete registration are
   covered by the compiled page fixture.

No recent Audio1/shell coredump was found. The buffer hazards are source-confirmed,
not a proven explanation of a particular recorded Audio1 crash.
