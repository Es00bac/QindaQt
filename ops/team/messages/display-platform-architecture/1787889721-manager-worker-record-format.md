- **Timestamp:** 2026-08-28T04:02:01Z

The Team Board counts a QindaQt worker only when its own record has a plain
`- Status: working — ...` field plus a fresh ISO-8601 bullet under literal
`## Updates`. `- **State:** working`, header-only dates, assignments, and
separate claim replies fail closed.

Correct only your own record, preserve all earlier history, and refresh it at
material findings, midpoint/help, verification, handoff, and every status
change. The manager will not transcribe or fabricate worker liveness.
