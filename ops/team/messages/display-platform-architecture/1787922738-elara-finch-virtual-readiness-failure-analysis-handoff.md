## 1. What the immutable failure is

Exact mechanism, all anchors at `3320afdb`:

1. `tests/session/desktop_session_runtime.py:299-309` `sample()` line 303 `current.wait(timeout=min(1.0, remaining))` waits for the **previous** probe using the **outer** readiness remainder from `await_complete_snapshot` (`:158`). The previous probe is still in `QThread::msleep(250)` after its marker (`tests/session/desktopsessionprobe.cpp:149-152`), so once the remainder drops below 0.25 s the wait raises `subprocess.TimeoutExpired` — here after 0.0718 s (`sandbox.log`).
2. `TimeoutExpired` is not a `RuntimeError`; `await_complete_snapshot` (`:143-165`) does not catch it, `last_pending` is dropped, and `test_desktop_session_nested.py:316-328` prints the generic `Command '[...probe]' timed out …`. Two further deadline exits also drop the reason (`:305`, `:284`); only `:157`/`:160` keep it.
3. `:280-288` consumes each stdout snapshot without archiving it; `:264-271` logs stderr only → 52 empty attempt logs.
4. Teardown happened and was authenticated: `run_inner` `finally` (`:395-396`) ran `_cleanup`; had it raised, the `ProcessContractError` text would have replaced the `TimeoutExpired` text. The per-role ledger is nonetheless discarded on the failure path (`:397-403` only on success). Kernel fact: bwrap exited at 12:45:07.108Z, ≈40 ms after the exception, so the PID namespace collapsed; the 55 s outer deadline (`test_desktop_session_nested.py:256`) never engaged.

Timeline (UTC): probe-001 log 12:44:52.070; probe-052 12:45:06.944; 52 attempts, mean spacing 0.286 s (250 ms probe sleep + start-up); deadline ≈12:45:07.07.

## 2. Product defect versus harness defect

Replaying the pure validator `_snapshot_pending` (3320afdb `desktop_session_runtime.py:75-140`) over the 51 archived real snapshots of run `26e772f2` (no product, session, bus, compositor, UI, endpoint, or host action; `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`):

- Every other cause is a **harness/documentation defect**: the readiness predicate at `3320afdb` can never be satisfied by the real compositor for three independent reasons (P1-2, P1-3, P1-4). With all three corrected, attempt **2** (+0.27 s after probe-001, ≈0.85 s after bwrap start) and all 49 later attempts satisfy the predicate: the contained desktop — compositor, private bus, Settings1/Audio1/Notifications owned, two mapped and committed `scope=dock` surfaces with `processId "76"` = shell, Text Editor `org.qindaqt.TextEditor`, Settings mapped with title `QindaQt Settings — Notifications` — was fully ready for ≈14.9 s of the 15 s window. Boot latency is not the problem; the 15 s cap has >50× margin on this host.
- The 72 ms deadline inheritance (P1-1) is real but is a symptom: with a correct predicate the loop would have returned on attempt 2 and never reached the deadline edge.

### P2

- **P2-3 (tests) — hostile coverage gaps.** `e325f2f1 test_desktop_session_readiness_unit.py` (3 tests) does not pin: the exact regression (previous probe alive with a sub-lifetime outer remainder must not raise `TimeoutExpired`); a marker-less probe; a marker-then-hang probe; a `Popen`-faked `_await_runtime_snapshot`; a killed probe appearing in the ledger; the outer deadline elapsing *during* the previous-probe wait still naming `last_pending`.
- **P2-4 (determinism; Rhea `1787922345`) — deriving `Virtual-<any index>` from the inventory weakens the "one immutable topology" contract** (ADR-0026:72-73) and accepts leaked backend state (`Virtual-17` on a single-output backend means seventeen prior output constructions). Also require `len(devices) == 1`: the S1 row claims "no host input node" (`testing-harness.md:970`) but only counts development devices.
- **P2-5 (probe/design) — unbounded D-Bus calls and no timeout taxonomy.** `desktopsessionprobe.cpp:109-144` never calls `setTimeout` (QtDBus default 25 s per call, five calls). A per-call timeout of 1 s should be set, reported distinctly as `dbus-call-timeout`, and treated as *pending* (retry until the cap), while every error *reply* (UnknownMethod, malformed JSON) stays immediate — a refinement of ADR-0026:83-85 (decision Q1).

- **R2 — readiness loop (Rhea, `desktop_session_readiness.py`/`desktop_session_runtime.py`).** Fixed total cap 15 s unchanged. Fixed per-attempt lifetime `L` (recommend 3 s; 1 s is acceptable only with the retry semantics below). A lifetime expiry before the marker records pending `attempt N produced no snapshot within L s` and retries while ≥ L remains; the previous probe's exit wait is bounded by its own remaining lifetime, never by the outer remainder; a minimum inter-attempt spacing of 200 ms bounds the attempt count. Every exception raised inside `sample` is re-raised as `RuntimeError("desktop topology readiness failed: <last_pending>; <cause>")` with the cause chained. The accepted probe's identity is captured inside `sample` immediately after its marker.
- **R3 — probe (Rhea, `desktopsessionprobe.cpp`).** Remove the internal 15 s service wait (pure sampler; the loop already retries `unavailable`); `compositor.setTimeout(1000)` and report `dbus-call-timeout` distinctly; replace `msleep(250)` by blocking on stdin until EOF (internal maximum 10 s) so the harness releases a probe by closing its stdin — pending probes immediately after their marker, the accepted probe after `_authenticate_processes` and PSS sampling.
